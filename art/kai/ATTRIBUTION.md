Kai character design/reference: provided by the user.

Character base geometry and source: user's Historiando project, derived from the existing editable Yara/chibi base. Kai-specific hair, clothes, materials and accessories were constructed locally in Blender.

The inherited base rig follows the project's earlier Gobot adaptation. Gobot by GDQuest, CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/gdquest-demos/godot-4-3D-Characters/tree/8034d3fede52c8a58d2cbc7b8d3e8e6c79f6c8b3

Earlier project changes: human/chibi proportions, finger hinges, joint-volume weights, and retargeted motion ranges. Kai retains the adapted base skeleton. Idle, Walk, Run, Jump, Fall and VictorySign were sampled from the project's compatible Yara adaptation, then adjusted for high boots and elbow hinges. Clothing and accessory paint, curved elbow cushions, six cinematic gestures and eight facial shape keys were authored locally for Kai.
